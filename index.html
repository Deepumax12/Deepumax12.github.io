Published

Marangoni boundary layers on surfactant-laden bubbles

Physics of Fluids 38, 102108 (2026)

Axisymmetric simulations of rising bubbles carrying insoluble surfactant, using a coupled volume-of-fluid and phase-field method with adaptive mesh refinement. From a large parametric campaign I derived a scaling law for the thickness of the Marangoni boundary layer, which matches the simulations to about 7% r.m.s., and backed it with domain-size sensitivity tests.

Bo 0.5–10 · Ga 5–140 · Ma 0–15 · Pes = 100

Accepted

Tension-raising solutes on a rising bubble

FMFP 2026, NIT Rourkela · 10–12 December 2026

Most surfactants lower surface tension, but some solutes raise it. I compared the two cases with direct numerical simulation and looked at how each one changes the wake, the velocity along the interface and the bubble's terminal rise velocity.

Ga 100 · Bo 0.5 · sweep in Ma
